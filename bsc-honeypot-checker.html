Why BNB Chain has so many honeypots

Cheap deployment and fast blocks made BNB Chain the busiest playground for token scams. A new honeypot can be deployed for a couple of dollars, shilled in a group, and abandoned once enough buyers are trapped. The good news: because BSC contracts are simple and their sells are cheap to simulate, they are also the easiest to screen.

What gets checked on BSC

  • Live sell simulation via honeypot.is — a real buy-then-sell on BNB Chain, so a "buys work, sells revert" trap is caught immediately.
  • Buy and sell tax — BSC scam tokens love a 99% sell tax that looks like 5% until you try to exit.
  • Owner powers — pausable trading, blacklist functions, mintable supply, hidden owners.
  • Liquidity — PancakeSwap pool size and whether it looks pullable.

FAQ

How do I check a BSC token for a honeypot?

Paste the BNB Chain contract address above with the network set to BNB Chain. The tool runs a live sell simulation and reads contract, holder and liquidity data to flag honeypots and rug risks.

Why are there so many honeypots on BNB Chain?

Deployment is cheap and fast, so scammers mass-produce tokens that let you buy but block the sell. Always simulate a sell before buying a new BSC token.

Is the BSC checker free?

Yes, it runs in your browser on public APIs with no account or wallet connection.
