What this honeypot checker does

Before you buy a new token, three things decide whether you can get your money back out: can you actually sell, can the developer change the rules after you buy, and is the liquidity real. This tool checks all three in seconds:

  • Sell simulation — an on-chain buy-then-sell is simulated (via honeypot.is on Ethereum, BSC and Base) to catch tokens that let you buy but block or heavily tax the sell.
  • Contract security flags — GoPlus data reveals mintable supply, proxy contracts, hidden owners, blacklist/whitelist functions, trading cooldowns and ownership that can be reclaimed after being renounced.
  • Holders & liquidity — holder count, top-holder concentration and DEX liquidity, so a token held by three wallets with $200 of liquidity is obvious at a glance.

How to check if a token is a honeypot

Copy the token's contract address from DexScreener, Etherscan/BscScan, or wherever you found the token. Paste it above and press Check. Leave the network on Auto-detect and it will find the right chain from the token's liquidity pools. Read the verdict: green means no mechanical scam was found, amber means treat with caution, red means the contract has traits that let it trap or drain buyers.

A green result is not a buy signal

This tool filters out mechanical scams — honeypots, hidden mints, pullable liquidity. It cannot tell you whether a token has any future, whether the team will abandon it, or whether the price will collapse because nobody cares. Plenty of tokens pass every automated check and still go to zero. Size your position accordingly and never invest more than you can lose.

FAQ

What is a honeypot token?

A token you can buy but cannot sell. The contract only lets the creator sell, or makes every other sell revert or get taxed at nearly 100%. Your money goes in and cannot come out.

Is this checker free?

Yes — it runs entirely in your browser on public APIs. No account, no wallet connection, no fee.

Which blockchains are supported?

Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism and Avalanche here; TON jettons in the bot. Sell simulation is available on Ethereum, BSC and Base; on other chains you still get full contract, holder and liquidity analysis.
